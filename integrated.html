Root cause — import-risk ownership break

  • Previous C&F provider SANUR AGENCY (also handled Fruits) removed due to Fruits issues.
  • Akij Logistics C&F appointed after F&B/Fruits separation without a developed transition control for F&B-specific assessment risks.

Fix: dedicated F&B procurement/import specialist · F&B-specialist C&F provider · Corporate Affairs Customs/BSTI protocol · pre-shipment clearance-readiness meeting for every import.
