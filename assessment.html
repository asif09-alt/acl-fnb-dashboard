Why F&B margin is unreliable

  • WH 10738 physical stock ≈ BDT 3.40 Cr (253 F&B SKUs) is NOT on the books — PC 818 Finished-Goods GL shows only 0.80M (2 rows, incomplete posting).
  • Purchases are expensed to COGS at GRN without inventory capitalization → COGS overstated, GM distorted.
  • True landed cost (duty / FX / freight / C&F / port) is not captured at SKU level — costing tables exist (6,744 rows BU-wide) but are bulk-commodity dominated and not F&B-isolable.
Fix order: inventory valuation & reconciliation → landed cost → contribution → pricing.

0–6 months

  • Quality hold → release workflow
  • True landed cost (shipment → charges → SKU)
  • Inventory GL valuation & ERP↔GL reconciliation
  • AR subledger + credit limits + collections
  • Pricing rules with margin floor + approval
  • RBAC / SoD / approval matrix · audit depth · data cleansing
